# Northwind Employee Handbook (sample document)

## Leave policy
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave accrues monthly and up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Sick leave is 10 days per year and a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time. Employees working remotely must be reachable on Slack during core hours.

## Expenses
Business travel must be approved in advance. Meals are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day while travelling. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase, with receipts attached.

## Probation
New hires complete a 3 month probation period. A review meeting takes place at the end of month 2, and the final confirmation decision is communicated in writing by the end of month 3.
