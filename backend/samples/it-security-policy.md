# Northwind IT Security Policy (sample document)

## Passwords and access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and the code repository. Passwords must be rotated only when a compromise is suspected.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Personal devices may access email only through the approved mobile app. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT desk within 2 hours.

## Incident reporting
Any suspected phishing or data exposure must be reported to security@northwind.example immediately. The security team will acknowledge within 1 hour during business hours and classify the incident as low, medium or high severity.

## Data retention
Customer records are retained for 7 years after contract end. Server logs are retained for 90 days. Backups are encrypted and tested for restore every quarter.
